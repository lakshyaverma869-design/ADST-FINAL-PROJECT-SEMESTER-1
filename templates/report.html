12. Limitations

Training labels are synthetic; there are no measured charging sessions, live EV counts, citywide traffic counts, or historical traffic records. Open-Meteo provides modelled/reanalysis weather. TomTom traffic, when configured, reflects a nearest-road segment rather than the whole city. Random test metrics should not be interpreted as real-world performance.

13. Future Scope

Incorporate permitted real traffic and charging-session data, validate on later time periods, and compare the current Random Forest against simple baselines. A separate traffic-feed integration could improve estimates after its coverage and cost are checked.
